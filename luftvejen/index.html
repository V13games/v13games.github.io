About the game

A road that winds forever through the sky, and a little car that never stops. Hold to turn right, let go to turn left, and try not to drift over the edge.

The road changes world as you go, from meadow to beach, icebergs and candy land, and each has its own grip. Grab faster cars, magnets, shields and coins on the way, and spend coins to keep a run going.

How to play

  1. Hold the button to turn right. Let go to turn left.
  2. Take corners cleanly for perfect turns and a bigger combo.
  3. Drive through a new car on the road to swap into it for the rest of the run.
  4. Make a player for each person, and race for the top of the leaderboard on this device.
