About the game

Light crawls across the Slowwater Strait at 70 knots, so everything you see has already happened. Enemy ships show where they were, not where they are, and your orders travel out at the same slow speed.

Read the amber delay rings, guess where the enemy has gone, and aim where your shells will land. Hold the lighthouses for points, or sink the enemy flagship to win outright.

How to play

  1. Click a ship, then click the sea to send it there. Drag to pick several ships.
  2. Give orders with F for a salvo, T for a torpedo, E to dazzle, R for a relay buoy and X to stop.
  3. Each lighthouse you hold scores a point a second. First to 700 wins, or sink the enemy flagship.
  4. New to it? Start with the guided tutorial.
