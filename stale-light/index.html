In the Slowwater Strait, light crawls at 70 knots. Everything you see has already happened.

  • Everything you see is old. Amber rings mark each 5 seconds of delay from your flagship and lighthouses.
  • Orders move at light speed. A ship acts when the ring reaches it, and you see it obey later still.
  • Lighthouses are wired. Hold one and you see and speak from it instantly. Each scores 1 point a second.

First to 700 points wins, or sink the enemy flagship.

Click a ship, then click the sea to send it. Drag to select several. Scroll to zoom, WASD or arrows to pan, Space to pause.

Bridge notes

Your ships

FlagshipYour eyes. It sees everything first and takes orders instantly. Lose it and you lose the war. BattleshipLong-range salvos. Aim where the target will be when the shells land. DestroyerTorpedoes give off no light, so nobody sees them coming. Can dazzle. CutterFastest at taking lighthouses. Can dazzle and drop relay buoys.

Reading the sea

ChevronsWhere a ship probably is now, if it held course. Dashed lineAn order still in transit. It turns solid when you see the ship obey. DazzleThe enemy sees the ship carry straight on while it really turns.

Click a ship, then click or right-click the sea to send it. Keys: 1 to 8 pick a ship, Q picks all but the flagship, Shift adds to the selection or queues a route. F, T, E, R, X give orders. Esc cancels.
