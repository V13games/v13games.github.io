How to play

W A S D or arrows: run
Space: jump
Shift: dive
Click game: lock mouse look
Right-drag or Q E: orbit camera
Mouse wheel: zoom
Esc: pause
M: mute
F held: fast-forward once you are done
A / D: next bean while spectating

Each round only the first beans over the line go through. Fall off and you restart at your last checkpoint. On goo maps, if the goo has already swallowed your checkpoint, you are out. The maps are drawn at random. The last round is always the crown.
