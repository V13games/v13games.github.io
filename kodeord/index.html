About the game

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. After each guess the tiles change colour to show which letters are right, which are in the word somewhere else, and which are not in it at all.

There is a new daily word in Danish and one in English, plus practice games whenever you want more. Your streaks and statistics are kept on this device.

How to play

  1. Type a five-letter word and press Enter.
  2. Green: the right letter in the right spot. Yellow: in the word, but somewhere else. Dark: not in the word.
  3. You have six guesses. A new daily word comes every day.
  4. Switch between Danish and English at the top. Practice games do not count in your stats.
