About the game

Strafe Arena is a fast first-person shooter with cowboys. Red takes on blue in an abandoned city street, a building site or a maze of yellow rooms, and bots fill every empty slot.

Movement is half the game: bunny-hop, strafe in the air and slide around corners. The bots only know what they see and hear, so they sneak toward gunshots and search the map quietly. Practise against them alone, or share your party code and play with friends: the match runs in the host's browser, so the host keeps that tab open.

How to play

  1. Pick red or blue and a map. Press PLAY to take on bots on your own.
  2. To play with friends, read out the party code on the right or press COPY INVITE. They press Join and type it. The host can switch bots off.
  3. The host presses START, everyone looks over the map, then clicks to deploy.
  4. Hold Space to bunny-hop and slide with C while sprinting. First team to 30 kills wins.

Controls

Computer
WASD to move and the mouse to look. Left click fires, right click aims. Space jumps, Shift sprints, C crouches or slides, R reloads and G throws smoke.
Phone and tablet
Touch controls appear on iPad and phones: a joystick on the left, drag to look, and buttons to fire, aim, jump, crouch and reload.
