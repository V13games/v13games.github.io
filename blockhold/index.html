About the game

Blocky monsters pour out of a glowing portal and march down a winding lane toward your citadel. You are not a cursor here: you run around the field yourself with a blaster, knock enemies down, collect the coins they drop and spend them on towers along the lane.

Six towers each have their own job: Blaster, splashing Mortar, long-range Sniper, slowing Frost, Pylon boosts and chain-lightning Tesla, and every one has three levels. Towers run out of ammo and big brutes smash them, so you dash between them to reload and repair while runners, flyers, shielded walkers and bosses keep coming.

How to play

  1. Before each wave, open Build, pick a tower and place it on a grey pad. Start the wave early for bonus money.
  2. Shoot enemies yourself and walk over the coins they drop to collect them.
  3. Walk up to a tower that is empty or broken and hold Reload (free) or Repair to keep it fighting.
  4. Tap a tower to upgrade it, change its target or sell it. Survive all 20 waves to win.

Controls

Computer
WASD to move, mouse to aim (click to lock it), hold left click to shoot, Space to dash, R to reload, hold E at a tower, B to build, 1–6 to pick a tower, F for double speed.
Phone and tablet
Thumb on the left for a joystick, drag on the right to aim, hold Fire to shoot, plus Dash and Build buttons. Built for the iPad in landscape.
