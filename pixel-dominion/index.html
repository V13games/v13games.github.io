About the game

You start as a tiny patch of land on a big pixel map, next to hundreds of bots, AI nations and other players. Send your troops into free land and along your borders, time your attacks while your army regrows, and build cities, ports, factories, defence posts, SAM launchers and missile silos with the gold that comes in. Play on a real-coastline World or Europe, or on five generated maps.

Diplomacy makes it messy. Ask your neighbours to ally, send gold and troops to friends, mark targets, and break an alliance when it suits you, though everyone will see you as a traitor. Send boats across the sea, trade between ports and launch atom bombs, hydrogen bombs or a MIRV. Public games start every 10 minutes, or you can make a private lobby for friends.

How to play

  1. Click free land to pick your start, then click land next to your border to expand. Your troops spread along the whole border.
  2. The attack ratio (bottom left, or keys 1 and 2) sets how many troops each click sends. Troops grow back fastest when you’re about half full.
  3. Spend gold in the build bar: cities raise your troop cap, ports trade for gold, defence posts hold the line, silos launch nukes.
  4. Right-click (or long-press) a player to ally, chat, send emojis or donate. Hold most of the land to win.

Controls

Computer
Click to expand or attack, right-click for the menu, drag or WASD to move, scroll to zoom. Shift+scroll or 1 and 2 change the attack ratio, M shows the minimap.
Phone and tablet
Tap to expand or attack, long-press for the menu, drag to move and pinch to zoom.
