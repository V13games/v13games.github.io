About the game

Little voidlings crew a drifting space station. Crewmates fix wiring, swipe cards, clear asteroids and run scans, while a hidden impostor picks them off, sneaks through the vents and sabotages the lights, the reactor and the oxygen.

Watch the halls on the security cameras, then find a body or press the emergency button to call a meeting. Check the map of where you were, talk it over and vote someone out. Play against bots that talk and vote too, or online with friends: one of you hosts, and the others type the room code.

How to play

  1. Crewmates: walk to the glowing consoles on your list and press E to do each task. Anyone can watch the cameras in the Cams room.
  2. Impostors: your team’s names show in red. Kill when the cooldown runs out, hide in the vents and sabotage to split the crew up.
  3. Report a body or press the emergency button to call a meeting. MAP shows where you were, then chat and vote.
  4. The crew wins by finishing every task or voting out the impostors.

Controls

Computer
WASD or the arrow keys to move, Shift to sprint, E to use, R to report. Impostors: Q to kill, V to vent, F to sabotage.
Phone and tablet
A joystick in the lower left and buttons in the lower right. Made with the iPad in mind.
