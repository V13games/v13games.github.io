How to play

Fit the three tray pieces onto the 8×8 board in any order. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Pieces never rotate. The game ends when no remaining piece fits.

Drag a piece onto the board and let go, or tap a piece, then tap the board cell for its top-left corner.

Every placement that clears a line raises the combo by 1 before it scores. The combo resets after 3 placements in a row without a clear; the dots beside it show how many you have left. Cell points and the board-clear bonus aren't multiplied.

Keyboard: 1, 2, 3 select a tray piece. Esc pauses or goes back.
