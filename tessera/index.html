About the game

Three pieces wait in the tray. Fit them onto the 8×8 board in any order, and fill a whole row or column to clear it.

Pieces never rotate, so plan ahead. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move to build a combo. The game ends when nothing left in the tray will fit.

How to play

  1. Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece and then a cell.
  2. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  3. Clear lines on moves close together to raise your combo.
  4. Clear the whole board for a 300-point bonus.

Controls

Computer
Drag pieces with the mouse. 1, 2 and 3 pick a piece, Esc pauses.
Phone and tablet
Drag pieces with your finger, or tap a piece and then a cell.
